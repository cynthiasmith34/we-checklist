Lights & Exterior
Headlights, Tail Lights, Turn Signals Test all lenses — replace any that are dim, cracked, or out
Brake Lights & Hazard Lights Have a helper verify while you press the brake pedal
Mirrors (All) — No cracks, properly adjusted
Windshield — No chips, cracks, or obstructions Chips smaller than a quarter can usually be repaired
Wiper Blades & Washer Fluid
Horn — Audible and functioning
Interior & Safety Equipment
Seatbelts — All positions latch & retract properly
Dashboard Warning Lights — None active at startup If check-engine, oil, or battery lights are on — report immediately
Fire Extinguisher — Present, charged, within reach
First Aid Kit — Stocked and accessible
Registration & Insurance Card — In vehicle, current
Cargo Area — Tools secured, no loose hazards Unsecured tools are a safety risk in a sudden stop
Under the Hood
Engine Oil Level & Color Pull dipstick — oil should be between MIN/MAX marks; brown/black = needs change
Coolant / Antifreeze Level Check reservoir (not radiator cap) — should be between LOW and FULL
Battery Terminals — Clean, secure, no corrosion White/blue crust on terminals = corrosion; flag for service
Drive Belts — No cracking, fraying, or visible wear
No Visible Leaks under hood (oil, coolant, fluid)
Tires & Wheels

Record tread depth (32nds) & PSI for each corner. Minimum safe tread: 2/32". Recommended PSI on door jamb sticker.

Brakes & Under Vehicle
Brake Pedal — Firm feel, no spongy or low travel Pedal should not sink to floor; note any soft/pulsing feel
Brake Noise — No squealing, grinding, or pulling
Parking / Emergency Brake — Holds on incline
Shock Absorbers / Suspension — No bounce, clunk, or lean
Ground Clearance — No visible fluid puddles under vehicle Puddle at rear = fuel or coolant; front-center = AC condensate (normal)
Exhaust — No unusual smoke color or loud rattling Blue smoke = oil burning; white smoke = coolant; black = rich fuel mix
